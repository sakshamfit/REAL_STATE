# RUDRA — 3D EXPERIENCE

> A cinematic digital experience for Rudra Constructions & Suppliers.

---

# 01 — CORE IDEA

This is **not a traditional corporate website**.

The website should feel like the visitor is entering a continuously evolving architectural world.

### Reference Direction

Inspired by the feeling of premium luxury/architectural WebGL websites:

* Cinematic camera movement
* Scroll-controlled environments
* Extreme minimalism
* Large typography
* Spatial transitions
* Depth
* Architectural objects
* Controlled lighting
* Editorial composition
* Almost no traditional UI cards

### Experience

```text
DARKNESS
    ↓
CONSTRUCTION SITE
    ↓
FOUNDATION
    ↓
BUILDING
    ↓
INTERIOR
    ↓
SERVICES
    ↓
CONSTRUCTION PROCESS
    ↓
INDIA
    ↓
PRESENCE
    ↓
RUDRA
    ↓
CONTACT
```

The entire website should feel like **one continuous shot**.

Continuous.

# 27 — PERFORMANCE

The experience must still work on Indian mobile devices.

Target:

```text
Desktop: 60 FPS
Mobile: 30+ FPS
```

Use:

```text
Lazy loading
GLB compression
Draco
KTX2
LOD
Instancing
Compressed textures
Asset streaming
Reduced shadows
```

Do not load every 3D scene at initial page load.

A 3D website should have a static/low-capability path because heavy WebGL experiences can otherwise become unusable on mobile devices.

---

# 28 — MOBILE

Mobile is NOT the desktop experience scaled down.

Create a mobile composition.

```text
Desktop
Camera movement
Large environment
High detail

Mobile
Reduced geometry
Reduced particles
Shorter camera paths
Touch interaction
Simplified lighting
```

Support:

```text
prefers-reduced-motion
```

If motion is disabled:

```text
3D still frames
+
simple transitions
+
full HTML content
```

# 30 — FINAL ART DIRECTION

Imagine the visitor saying:
