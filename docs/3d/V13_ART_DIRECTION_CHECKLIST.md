# V13 Art Direction Checklist — RUDRA CONSTRUCTIONS & SUPPLIERS

> Per-shot visual quality audit. Each major beat scored on 9 dimensions.
> Any shot below 8.5 must be fixed.

---

## Scoring Key
- **COMPOSITION** — Rule of thirds, leading lines, visual hierarchy
- **DEPTH** — Foreground/midground/background separation
- **MATERIAL** — Surfaces look real, not procedural
- **LIGHTING** — Photographic, not CG
- **ENVIRONMENT** — Coherent world, not isolated objects
- **SCALE** — Human-scale cues present
- **CAMERA** — Cinematic language, not robotic
- **STORY** — Visual narrative clear without text
- **PHOTOREALISM** — Would pass as architectural commercial frame
